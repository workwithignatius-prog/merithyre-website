Is there a contract?

No. There's no long-term contract on any plan and no annual commitment.

How does per-recruiter pricing work for agencies?

Agencies pay $99 per recruiter per month with every pipeline feature included. A five-recruiter desk is $495 a month.

Are there per-role or per-hire fees?

No. You pay the plan price, with no per-role or per-hire fees.

Do we need an ATS?

No. MeritHyre runs standalone, or syncs with Bullhorn, Greenhouse and Tellent. Custom integrations are available on Enterprise.

How long does setup take?

Under a day. Connect your ATS (or don't), configure a role, and the pipeline starts working right away.

Is volume pricing available?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. Tell us about your hiring volume and SLA needs and we'll put a quote together.
